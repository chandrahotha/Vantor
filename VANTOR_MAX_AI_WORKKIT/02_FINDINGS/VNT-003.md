# VNT-003 — Direct invoice approval route bypasses the approval queue and role authority.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** High
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Authorization / financial control

## Location

`backend/app/routers/purchase.py:455-480`

## Evidence

`approve_invoice()` calls `_write(actor)` at line 457, checks only SoD at line 464, then immediately performs the 3-way match and sets `inv.status = "approved"` at line 469. It does not require an `Approval` row or `APPROVER_ROLES` membership.

## Impact

Invoice payment authorization can be performed outside the intended human approval workflow and without tiered financial authority.

## Required remediation

Create the invoice approval row when invoice enters the approval state. Route approval through the shared approval service. Keep 3-way match as a prerequisite, not an alternative approval system. Record authorization details and enforce amount thresholds.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Role matrix; invoice creator cannot approve; finance-only invoice approval; multi-tier invoice approval; no approval row => 409/422; concurrent approval.
