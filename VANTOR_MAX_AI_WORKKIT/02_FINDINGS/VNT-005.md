# VNT-005 — Receipt cumulative quantity check is race-prone.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** High
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Concurrency / inventory

## Location

`backend/app/routers/purchase.py:383-416`

## Evidence

Prior receipts are read at lines 395-402. The new receipt is then flushed and the application checks `prior + quantity > ordered` at line 410. No row-level lock protects the PO line or receipt aggregate from concurrent writers.

## Impact

Concurrent receipts can over-receive a PO line even though each request independently passes the check.

## Required remediation

Serialize receipt operations by PO line, preferably locking all affected PO lines in deterministic order. Recalculate the cumulative quantity after locking and before insert/commit.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Concurrent receipts on same line; multiple lines in opposite order to detect deadlocks; exact boundary; retry after deadlock.
