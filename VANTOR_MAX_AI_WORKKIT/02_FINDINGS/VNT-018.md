# VNT-018 — Critical lifecycle states are defined but not consistently reachable through first-class APIs.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** High
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Workflow integrity

## Location

`backend/app/models/purchase.py:20-23; backend/app/routers/purchase.py:206-480`

## Evidence

Model statuses include PO `closed/cancelled`, Invoice `matched/paid/rejected`, but the live purchase router exposes no first-class endpoints to close/cancel PO or reject/pay invoices. Invoice approval also bypasses Approval rows.

## Impact

State machine semantics are incomplete, causing operational dead ends and encouraging direct database/state mutation or ad-hoc behavior.

## Required remediation

Define an explicit transition registry per aggregate; expose commands for every valid transition and no others. Require reason/audit/context for rejection/cancellation/closure/payment. Prevent direct arbitrary status updates.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Transition matrix exhaustive test; invalid transition; duplicate command; concurrent transition; idempotent replay.
