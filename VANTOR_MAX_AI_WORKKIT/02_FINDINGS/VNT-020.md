# VNT-020 — RFQ evaluation gate does not enforce quote completeness, minimum quote count, bid validity, or supplier eligibility.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** Medium-High
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Sourcing business logic

## Location

`backend/app/routers/sourcing.py:160-180; 183-232`

## Evidence

`move_rfq()` can mark an RFQ evaluated and mark all submitted quotes evaluated without checks that required RFQ lines are covered, minimum quote count is met, bid validity is active, supplier is qualified, or currencies are consistent.

## Impact

An RFQ can reach award readiness without satisfying important sourcing controls.

## Required remediation

Create an evaluation service that computes eligibility/completeness and records an immutable evaluation snapshot before allowing award. Make business exceptions explicit and audited.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

0 quotes; 1 quote; missing line; expired quote; unqualified supplier; mixed currencies; partial quote.
