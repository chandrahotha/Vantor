# VNT-026 — Supplier qualification decision authority is broader than a specialized approval role.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Supplier governance

## Location

`backend/app/routers/suppliers.py:410-433`

## Evidence

`decide_qual()` uses `_require_write(actor)` and only prevents the submitter from deciding. It does not require a supplier-risk/compliance authority role.

## Impact

Qualification is a gate for downstream sourcing but is controlled by generic write access.

## Required remediation

Define a supplier qualification permission and optional dual review. Require rejection reasons, immutable decision snapshots, and evidence completeness.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Role matrix; reject reason; missing evidence; self-approval; concurrent decisions.
