# VNT-033 — Metrics are process-local and not suitable as the primary production telemetry system.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Observability

## Location

`backend/app/core/observe.py:22-47`

## Evidence

Counters and latency histograms are stored in module-level Python structures and exposed by one process. OTEL is disabled by default and documented as future work.

## Impact

Horizontal replicas fragment metrics and restarts erase history; operators lack distributed traces and durable service-level telemetry.

## Required remediation

Emit OpenTelemetry traces/metrics/logs with service identity, tenant-safe dimensions, latency/error histograms, queue metrics, DB pool metrics, and SLO dashboards. Keep local metrics only as a diagnostic fallback.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Two replicas; correlation across API/worker; trace propagation; redaction.
