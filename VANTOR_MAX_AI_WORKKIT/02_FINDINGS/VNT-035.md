# VNT-035 — Container dependencies use mutable tags instead of immutable image digests.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Supply chain

## Location

`docker-compose.yml:1-80`

## Evidence

Examples include `minio/minio:latest`, `ollama/ollama:latest`, `redis:7-alpine`, and `quay.io/keycloak/keycloak:25.0`.

## Impact

The same deployment specification can resolve to different image content over time, reducing reproducibility and complicating incident response.

## Required remediation

Pin production images by digest, generate SBOMs, verify signatures/provenance, scan images, and promote immutable image artifacts through environments.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Policy check rejects latest/mutable tags in production manifest.
