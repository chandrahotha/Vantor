# Currency Tax Fx

Use an ISO-4217 registry and explicit tenant-supported currencies. Store currency on every monetary aggregate. Never sum different currencies. FX conversions require source, rate, timestamp, base/quote currency and rounding policy. Tax must be modeled separately from net/gross where the workflow requires it.
