# Information Architecture

Target navigation:

**Overview**: Executive dashboard, work queue, alerts.

**Source & Suppliers**: Suppliers, Supplier 360, RFQs, awards, negotiation workspace.

**Contracts**: Contract repository, renewals, obligations, signatures.

**Procure-to-Pay**: Requisitions, Approvals, Purchase Orders, Receipts, Invoices.

**Intelligence**: Spend, Price Cases, Risk, Should-Cost, Analytics.

**Documents & AI**: Documents, Evidence, Copilot, AI Control Center.

**Integrations**: ERP/API/Webhooks/Jobs.

**Administration**: Users/Roles, Policies, Categories, Currency/Region, Audit, System health.

Do not bury approvals inside Copilot; make the approval center first-class.
