# VNT-022 — Contract status transitions and signing are authorized by broad write roles rather than specific authority.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** High
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Authorization / contracts

## Location

`backend/app/routers/contracts.py:34,151-167,221-251`

## Evidence

`WRITE_ROLES` includes Buyer and other roles. `move_contract()` only calls `_write()`. `sign_contract()` also only calls `_write()` and allows internal or e-sign sign-off based on input validation.

## Impact

Users with broad write rights may perform lifecycle or legal actions beyond their intended authority.

## Required remediation

Create contract-specific permissions: edit draft, submit review, legal approve, sign, activate, terminate, renew. Tie signing authority to spend/value thresholds, legal role, SoD and immutable contract version snapshots.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

Role matrix; value thresholds; signer SoD; invalid state; changed-after-signature; termination/renewal authority.
