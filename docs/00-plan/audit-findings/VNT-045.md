# VNT-045 — Key procurement actions are fragmented or missing dedicated operational surfaces in the current navigation.

> **Source of truth:** Current VANTOR archive re-audit

## Classification

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Status:** Verified
- **Confidence:** High
- **Category:** Frontend workflow

## Location

`frontend/app route inventory; frontend/components/Shell.tsx; frontend/app/shared/orders.tsx and copilot approvals`

## Evidence

There are 14 page routes and approval UI is embedded under Copilot. Purchase orders and invoices share a generic Orders surface, while there is no dedicated Approval Center or Invoice workspace route in the current page inventory.

## Impact

Daily users lack a clear task-oriented operating model and important controls are buried inside broad pages.

## Required remediation

Rework information architecture around Work Queue, Approvals, Sourcing, Contracts, Procure-to-Pay, Supplier 360, Spend Intelligence, Documents, AI Control Center, Admin/Settings. Give invoices and approvals first-class detail/workbench views.

## Required invariant / acceptance

The remediation must preserve tenant isolation, auditability, authorization correctness and deterministic behavior.

## Required tests

User journeys for buyer/approver/finance/supplier manager; keyboard and deep-link routing.
