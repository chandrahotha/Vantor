# Build Order for Products 06–10

Build in this sequence after Products 01–05 exist as stable contracts:

1. Product 06 — Strategic Sourcing Optimization Engine
2. Product 07 — Procurement Spend Intelligence Agent
3. Product 08 — Supplier Onboarding & Qualification Agent
4. Product 09 — PO Price Intelligence Engine
5. Product 10 — AI Supplier Negotiation Simulator

Why this order: sourcing optimization consumes the richest analytical inputs; spend intelligence becomes a useful upstream signal source; onboarding stabilizes supplier master quality; PO price intelligence adds transactional price control; the negotiation simulator then consumes the strongest sourcing/cost/supplier evidence without touching production decisions.

Each product must first pass its standalone release gate before portfolio integration work begins.
