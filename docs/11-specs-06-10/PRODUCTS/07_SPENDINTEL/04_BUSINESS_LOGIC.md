# Business Logic — Procurement Spend Intelligence Agent

1. Only successfully reconciled transactions enter the published spend cube.
2. Classification confidence below threshold moves to review.
3. Savings opportunities must identify a measurable baseline and calculation basis.
4. Duplicate detection must not double-count spend.
5. Tax/currency normalization preserves source values and conversion basis.
6. Maverick spend rules are versioned and explainable.
7. AI may propose classification candidates but cannot silently publish authoritative classifications.
