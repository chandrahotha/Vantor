# Business Logic — PO Price Intelligence Engine

1. Price comparison begins with comparability, not scoring.
2. Explicit states: COMPARABLE, PARTIAL, NOT_COMPARABLE.
3. Baselines use frozen peer definitions, time window, currency and UOM basis.
4. An anomaly is a deterministic result against versioned thresholds/statistics, not an AI guess.
5. Leakage is shown as a decomposition with evidence.
6. Dismissals require reason and are auditable.
7. Stale or insufficient history lowers confidence or blocks a definitive anomaly classification.
