## When this document applies

**This agreement does not apply to self-hosted use of VANTOR.** Downloading
and running Apache-2.0 licensed software is governed by the license and by
`terms-of-service.md`, not by a services agreement — there is no vendor
relationship to document.

An MSA becomes relevant only if Digi Tracks sells one or more of: paid
support, professional services (implementation, customization, training), or
a hosted/managed offering, under a negotiated commercial agreement rather than
click-through Terms of Service. `[TO CONFIRM: does Digi Tracks offer any of
these today, or is this forward-looking?]`

## If Digi Tracks offers paid services, this MSA needs real terms for:

1. **Parties and term** — the contracting entity (`[TO CONFIRM: legal entity
   name]`) and the initial/renewal term of the agreement. `[TO CONFIRM]`
2. **Scope of services** — what is actually sold (support tier, professional
   services, hosting, SLA). `[TO CONFIRM]`
3. **Fees and payment terms** — pricing, invoicing cadence, payment method,
   late-payment consequences. `[TO CONFIRM — do not publish invented pricing]`
4. **Service levels**, if any (uptime commitment, support response times,
   credits for missed targets). `[TO CONFIRM]`
5. **Intellectual property** — VANTOR's source remains Apache-2.0 licensed
   regardless of any services sold around it; this section should clarify
   that a services agreement does not change the software's license, and
   should state ownership of any custom work product. `[TO CONFIRM: how
   bespoke/custom work is licensed]`
6. **Confidentiality** — mutual obligations for information exchanged under
   the agreement (which is likely to include the customer's own business data
   if Digi Tracks provides implementation or support services).
7. **Warranties and disclaimers** — any service-level warranty beyond the
   software's own "AS IS" terms. `[TO CONFIRM]`
8. **Liability** — a liability cap tied to fees paid is the common structure;
   the actual cap and carve-outs need legal input. `[TO CONFIRM]`
9. **Termination** — for convenience and for cause, including notice periods
   and effect on any hosted data (see `data-processing-agreement.md`).
   `[TO CONFIRM]`
10. **Governing law and dispute resolution.** `[TO CONFIRM]`
